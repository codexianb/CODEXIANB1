Free Random Video Chat with Strangers

Codexianb is a free random video chat site for adults 18 and older. Press Start and you are matched with a stranger from anywhere in the world, or from a country you choose. You can also use text-only chat.

How it works

  • Confirm you are 18 or older.
  • Allow your camera and microphone for video chat.
  • Press Start to be matched with a random person.
  • Skip to the next person at any time.

Safety

Every chat has a Report button. Users reported by several different people are banned automatically, and repeat offenders face longer bans. Never share personal or financial information with strangers. Read the Community Guidelines.

FAQ

Is Codexianb free? Yes, it is free to use.

Do I need an account? No. There is no signup.

Who can use it? Adults aged 18 and older only.

Can I choose a country? Yes. Pick a country before you start, or leave it on Any.

What if someone breaks the rules? Press Report. Users reported by several different people are banned.
