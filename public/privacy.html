Privacy Policy

Draft based on how the app currently works. Have a lawyer review it before relying on it.

What we collect

  • Network address (IP), hashed. Used only to enforce bans and rate limits and to count reports. We store a salted hash, not the raw address.
  • Reports and bans. Who was reported (by hash), when, and the ban status.
  • Browser storage. Your age confirmation and preferred country are saved in your browser.

What we do not store

Video and audio are not recorded or stored by us. Text messages are passed between users through our server and are not saved.

Third parties

  • Metered provides relay (TURN/STUN) servers so calls can connect on strict networks.
  • Render hosts our server.
  • Netlify hosts this website.

During a call, your IP address is visible to the other person's browser as part of how peer-to-peer video works.

Your choices

You can clear your browser storage at any time. Contact us to ask about data tied to a ban.

Age

This service is for people 18 and older only.
