Community Guidelines

Codexianb is for adults 18+. Be respectful. Breaking these rules can get you banned.

Not allowed

  • Nudity, sexual content or sexual solicitation.
  • Anyone under 18 using the service, or any content involving minors.
  • Harassment, hate speech, threats or bullying.
  • Recording or sharing another person's video, audio or chat without consent.
  • Scams, spam, advertising or asking for money.
  • Illegal activity of any kind.

Reporting and bans

Use the Report button during a chat. When several different users report the same person, that person is banned: 24 hours first, 7 days second, then permanent. We may also ban manually.

Stay safe

Do not share your full name, address, phone number, passwords or payment details. Leave any chat that makes you uncomfortable.
